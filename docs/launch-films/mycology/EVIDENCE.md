## Capture acceptance boundaries

- Product records are local and owner-entered. No sensor ingestion, scale integration, biological interpretation, food-safety decision, certification or regulator approval is demonstrated.
- Vision is unavailable in the current production setup and excluded from affirmative claims. No old phone take, synthetic gateway output or teaser replaces that missing capability.
- No terminal, code editor, developer repository or developer-mode lecture appears in the grower film. The app's own record details may be inspected; explain them in grower language. Do not cover a material warning or original fact for a cleaner frame.
- A clear form error is not an uncertain write. An uncertain write is not proof of failure. Preserve and reconcile original intents; no profile reset to manufacture continuity.
- No real contact address is needed. Shipments are fictional records and trigger no physical dispatch.
- Recovery is an orderly independent copy to empty destinations, not merging, automatic backup, cloud sync, full-profile transfer or physical power-loss testing.
- No archive footage is accepted. Optional later farm atmosphere requires rights, full used-range identity/specimen/label review and an explicit historical-context label. Invalidated biological take5/take9 readings remain excluded.
