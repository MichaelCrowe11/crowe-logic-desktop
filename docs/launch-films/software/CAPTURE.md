1. Release owner identifies one accepted Desktop or Developers artifact, its SHA-256, app ID, version and platform. Do not mix editions under one product name. Confirm customer-safe roster and profile/auth/session isolation acceptance.
2. A single capture owner uses a fresh disposable demo profile and a separate copy of `sample-project/`. Never open proprietary product source, the real home directory or a customer repository. Do not use the old film profile or old fixture-driven browser scene.
3. Record the source hashes of every sample file. The sample is deliberately defective and labelled as such; do not repair it before capture. No existing customer project is altered to manufacture a problem.
4. Validate the rig against the accepted artifact without changing its shipping behavior. Local DOM/compositor control is preferable to visible-screen automation. Do not launch the historical rig unchanged: its app, profile and workspace paths are historical and its reset/approval helpers are inappropriate here. Read its SHOTLIST/TREATMENT/SCRIPT before any later adaptation.
5. Confirm no visible desktop takeover, notifications or real account details. Select only the sample workspace. Show material mode/approval/engine disclosures honestly. Any synthetic account label must be editorial, not a claim of real authentication.
6. Verify approved runtime access and available allowance before any real model session. No calls in this package. Do not copy credentials into fixtures, receipts or footage.

## Take rules

- Record action, wait and result continuously before editorial trimming. Keep a wall-time log. Any removed latency receives a visible `Wait shortened` label outside product content.
- Select a frame rate from actual capture performance; preserve timing rather than claiming captured frame rate from an encoder flag. Hold critical output long enough to read at phone size.
- Keep failed takes in the private evidence record. A new coherent rehearsal gets a distinct profile/project copy and take ID. Do not splice different project states into one continuous action.
- Before each take record sample hashes and intended modes. After it, record hashes, actual actions and outcome. No broad resets or profile clearing during an unresolved operation.
- Reject any take exposing farm navigation/content, biological material, private prompts, account information or a material contradiction hidden by the crop.
- Stop at a missing permission, route or accepted artifact. Missing footage remains missing; no fixture substitution.

## Sample test contract

`sample-project/summary.cjs` intentionally counts archived rows and divides by zero on an empty list. Six tests specify active-set counting, partially complete work, empty/all-archived sets, rounding and input preservation. The initial test run is expected to fail four tests and pass two. This establishes a reproducible teaching problem only. No corrected implementation is supplied for an assistant to pretend to discover, and no app-generated repair exists yet.

The sample accepts only arrays of task records with `state` equal to `todo`, `done` or `archived`. Arbitrary imported data validation is outside its teaching contract. The production film must not generalize its six tests into broad software correctness.
