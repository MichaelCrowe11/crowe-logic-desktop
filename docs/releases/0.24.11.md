# Crowe Logic 0.24.11

Messages edition. Carries everything on main since 0.24.10:

- Conversations read as texts. A worker's bubbles sit on the left as raised cards with its mark beside the last bubble of a run; yours sit on the right, unnamed. Consecutive texts from one sender a few minutes apart group into a run. Sender names appear on the first bubble of a run, and only in a group (PR 100).
- Delivered and Read. "Delivered" or "Read" with the time sits under your last text and nowhere else. Read means a worker took it up.
- A typing bubble in the worker's own mark while it works, always the last thing in the thread.
- Your text lands in the thread the moment you send it, not when the worker's turn returns.
- Reactions. Hover a worker's bubble and pick a word: Good, More, No or Why. It sits on the bubble as a chip, gold because it is yours, and a second tap takes it off. The worker reads it on its next turn as a line after that message, so a "Why" is an instruction without a sentence (PR 101).
- Workers are named as colleagues, without the company in front: Operator, Studio Director, Orchestrator. The contact list, the rail, the thread and the phone all agree.
- A pill composer whose Send lights when there is text; dock tabs titled by the conversation instead of "Untitled room"; the New message list on the same grid.

Updates are served from https://releases.crowelogic.com/desktop/channel/<os>. macOS builds are notarized and stapled on the release machine. The Windows installer is signed through Azure Trusted Signing; SmartScreen shows the publisher as Michael Crowe until an organisation validation is added.
