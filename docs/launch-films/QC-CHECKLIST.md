## Voice and sound gate

- [ ] Spoken text matches locked paragraph hashes; headings, IDs and placeholders never spoken.
- [ ] Every paragraph listened to and transcript-checked for truncation, duplication, pronunciation and artifacts.
- [ ] Continuous Talon bed under the long-form narration, not the separate no-music lesson recipe.
- [ ] Approximately 12 LU voice/bed separation auditioned on voiced windows; ducking holds through short pauses.
- [ ] Drums muted under speech; intended rises only in speech-free hinges; no pumping or clipped transitions.
